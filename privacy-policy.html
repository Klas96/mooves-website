Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

The short version: mooves does its work entirely on your device. There's no account system, no server that receives your data, and your videos never leave your phone. We collect no personal data.

Data We Collect

None. mooves has no sign-up, no login, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, or share any personal information about you.

Your Videos and Audio

To analyze a dance, mooves reads a video you record or choose from your device. The video, its audio track, and the body-pose data extracted from it are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Temporary working files created during analysis are deleted from your device when the analysis finishes.

Permissions We Use

  • Camera — only if you choose to record a clip in the app. Captured video stays on your device.
  • Microphone — to capture the music while recording, so your moves can be matched to the beat. Audio stays on your device.
  • Photos / media / storage — to let you pick an existing video to analyze.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. mooves does not request location, contacts, or any advertising identifier.

Third-Party Services

mooves uses Google's on-device ML Kit pose-detection library, which runs locally and does not send your video off the device. The app is distributed through Google Play; your download and any optional purchases are handled by Google under their own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

mooves does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete — anything the app stores lives only on your own device and is removed when you uninstall it.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
